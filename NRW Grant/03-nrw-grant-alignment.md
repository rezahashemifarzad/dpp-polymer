## 3.1 How this project matches NRW's strategic goals

NRW's state government and NRW.BANK organize their innovation and Mittelstand funding around a small number of standing strategic priorities that this project maps onto directly rather than tangentially:

- **Sustainability and circular economy.** NRW has an explicit resource-efficiency and circular-economy funding line (Ressource.NRW) aimed at exactly the kind of material-flow transparency, recycled-content tracking, and resource-efficiency documentation this platform provides. A DPP platform that makes PCR/PIR content and mass-balance chain-of-custody auditable is infrastructure for the circular economy, not merely a beneficiary of unrelated sustainability funding.
- **Digitalization of traditional/industrial Mittelstand.** NRW.BANK's Mittelstand Innovativ & Digital (MID) program family exists specifically to fund digitalization projects in small and medium enterprises, including digitalization of a company's compliance and supply-chain data processes — this platform is, functionally, a digitalization-of-compliance product for the chemical/plastics Mittelstand.
- **EU regulatory readiness for regional industry.** NRW's chemical and plastics sector faces the ESPR/PPWR compliance deadline as an industry-wide, region-wide event, not a company-specific problem — the state has a direct interest in regional industry not falling behind competitors in other EU states/regions on DPP readiness, which is the explicit logic behind EFRE.NRW/JTF's "Gründung und Digitalisierung" (founding and digitalization) funding priority for the 2021–2027 structural-funds period.
- **Research-to-market translation via the regional science base.** NRW.BANK and EFRE.NRW programs consistently reward projects with a credible link to the state's research infrastructure (universities, Fraunhofer, application centers) as evidence of technical seriousness and translation potential — this project's fit with the RWTH Aachen ecosystem (Section 3.3) is a genuine differentiator on this criterion, not decoration.

## 3.2 Candidate NRW / EU funding instruments

The instruments below are grouped by what stage and type of activity they typically fund. All require, at minimum, a registered establishment (Betriebsstätte) in NRW and fall within defined SME (KMU) size thresholds for the most favorable terms — confirm the applicant entity's exact SME classification before selecting a program.

### NRW.BANK.Invest Zukunft
A financing/investment product from NRW.BANK explicitly targeted at transforming businesses digitally and sustainably ("Unternehmen digital und nachhaltig transformieren"). Fit: this is the most direct match for a SaaS platform whose entire value proposition is digital + sustainability transformation of an industrial customer base. [NRW.BANK.Invest Zukunft](https://www.nrwbank.de/de/foerderung/foerderprodukte/60274/nrwbank-invest-zukunft.html) · [Program summary, nordrhein-westfalen-foerdert.nrw](https://www.nordrhein-westfalen-foerdert.nrw/foerdermassnahmen/nrwbankinvest-zukunft-2026)

### Mittelstand Innovativ & Digital (MID) — Digitalisierung and MID (general)
NRW.BANK's core SME digitalization funding line, including a voucher (Gutschein)-based sub-track for smaller digitalization projects. Fit: relevant both as a funding source for the platform vendor itself (as an NRW-based digital innovator) and, separately, as a funding mechanism the platform's own prospective manufacturer *customers* could use to co-finance their adoption of the platform — worth surfacing to sales prospects as a customer-acquisition angle, not just as funding for this company. [MID overview](https://www.nrwbank.de/de/foerderung/foerderprodukte/60382/mittelstand-innovativ-und-digital-mid.html) · [MID – Digitalisierung Gutscheine](https://www.nrwbank.de/de/foerderung/foerderprodukte/60348/mittelstand-innovativ--digital-mid---digitalisierung.html) · [2026 program page](https://www.nordrhein-westfalen-foerdert.nrw/foerdermassnahmen/mittelstand-innovativ-digital-mid-digitalisierung-2026)

### EFRE/JTF-Programm NRW 2021–2027 — "Gründung und Digitalisierung"
The NRW implementation of the EU's European Regional Development Fund (ERDF/EFRE) and Just Transition Fund, with an explicit founding-and-digitalization funding priority for the current structural-funds period. Fit: this is the natural home for a growth-stage digital startup building regionally significant infrastructure, and it is EU co-financed, which is relevant framing for any pitch that positions the platform as EU regulatory infrastructure, not just a national product. [EFRE/JTF-Programm NRW 2021-2027](https://www.nordrhein-westfalen-foerdert.nrw/foerdermassnahmen/efrejtf-programm-nrw-2021-2027-schwerpunkt-gruendung-und-digitalisierung-2026) · [efre.nrw — Foundation and digitization](https://www.efre.nrw/en/understand/main-topics/foundation-and-digitization)

### Ressource.NRW (2025–2026)
A dedicated NRW resource-efficiency/circular-economy funding program run via the state's environmental/economic administration (EFA.NRW). Fit: the most directly on-theme program for a platform whose core function is recycled-content (PCR/PIR) tracking, mass-balance chain-of-custody, and material-flow transparency — this should be positioned as the primary circular-economy-specific application, distinct from the general digitalization programs above. [Förderprogramm Ressource.NRW 2025-2026](https://www.efa.nrw/fuer-unternehmen/angebote/beratung-finanzierung/foerderprogramm-ressourcenrw-2025-2026)

### Innovationsförderagentur NRW (IN.NRW)
The state's central coordination point for innovation funding across NRW, useful less as a single program and more as a first-contact advisory channel to identify which specific current call (including ones not yet public at the time of this document) best fits the project — recommended as a first step before committing to a specific application. [Innovationsförderagentur NRW](https://www.in.nrw/)

### General SME funding overview
NRW.BANK maintains a consolidated overview of its SME funding products for innovation, digitalization, and know-how transfer, useful as a single starting page to cross-check for newer or better-fitting programs than the ones named above. [Förderprogramme für KMU](https://www.nrwbank.de/de/unternehmen/nrw-bank-innovationspartner/foerderprogramme-kmu/)

## 3.3 RWTH Aachen ecosystem synergy

RWTH Aachen's **Center for Circular Economy (CCE)** is a standing, well-resourced research center explicitly organized around circular-economy research and industry translation — exactly the domain this platform digitizes. [RWTH Aachen — Center for Circular Economy](https://www.cce.rwth-aachen.de/) · [Research & Innovation at CCE](https://www.cce.rwth-aachen.de/cms/cce/Das-Center/~blstsa/Unsere-Forschung/lidx/1)

Concretely, this synergy supports the grant case in three ways:

1. **Technical validation.** A collaboration or advisory relationship with CCE (or an adjacent RWTH group such as the PEM Chair's circular-economy-and-materials work, or the Aufbruch excellence-cluster's circular-economy innovation area) gives grant reviewers independent, credible confirmation that the platform's emission-factor methodology, mass-balance modeling, and material-data schema reflect current materials-science practice — directly addressing the kind of scrutiny an NRW.BANK or EFRE.NRW technical review panel applies. [RWTH — Circular Economy Made in Aachen](https://magazines.rwth-aachen.de/en/keep-in-touch-73/wissenschaft-wirtschaft/circular-economy-made-in-aachen) · [Aufbruch — Innovation Area 2 (Circular Economy)](https://www.aufbruch.rwth-aachen.de/cms/aufbruch/innovationsbereiche/~bhvpzs/innovationsbereich-2/?lidx=1)
2. **Industry credibility by association.** RWTH Aachen already has active industry partnerships with major chemical/materials companies on circular-economy and sustainable-materials research (for example, Covestro's ongoing sustainable-materials research collaboration with RWTH Aachen), demonstrating that the region's flagship industrial chemical players already treat RWTH's circular-economy research as a serious partner — a platform aligned with the same ecosystem inherits some of that credibility with the same customer base. [Covestro and RWTH Aachen — sustainable materials research](https://www.pcimag.com/articles/112792-covestro-and-rwth-aachen-extend-pioneering-research-in-sustainable-materials)
3. **Talent and future R&D pipeline.** Proximity to RWTH Aachen's materials-science and circular-economy graduate programs is a practical talent-pipeline argument for the Domain-Expert function of this project's own team structure, and a plausible basis for a future joint research application (e.g. a Fraunhofer-affiliated applied-research grant) building on the DPP data the platform accumulates — flagged here as a Year 2+ opportunity, not part of the current ask.

**Recommendation:** before filing any grant application that cites this synergy, establish at minimum an informal advisory or letter-of-support relationship with a named RWTH Aachen contact (CCE or an equivalent group) — reviewers weight a named, real collaboration far higher than a general regional-proximity claim, and most NRW.BANK/EFRE.NRW application forms have a specific field for named partners/references.

## 3.4 Positioning summary for the application narrative

The strongest single-sentence framing for any of the above applications: *"Polymer DPP builds the digital infrastructure NRW's chemical and plastics Mittelstand needs to meet a legally mandated EU transparency requirement (ESPR/PPWR) that is already approaching, turning a compliance cost into an auditable trust asset — using a technical architecture (cryptographic immutability, GS1-standard QR verification, third-party auditor workflow) developed in NRW and validated against the region's own materials-science research base."* Each program-specific application in Section 3.2 should adapt this framing to that program's own specific priority language (digitalization vs. circular economy vs. regional structural development) rather than submitting identical text to each — reviewers for different programs are scoring against different published criteria.
